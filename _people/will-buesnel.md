Will completed a FUSE internship in 2026, funded by the Faraday Institution, focusing on better understanding how thermal properties propagate through to overall cell performance. This was done through a Bayesian sensitivity analysis on pre-existing ECMs and data obtained by the group to better quantify how parameters and operating regions of the model (SOC, temperature) contribute to overall error. Following this, he continued his MEng in Engineering Mathematics at the University of Bristol.
