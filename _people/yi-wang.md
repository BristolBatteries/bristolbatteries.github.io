Completed a 8-week research internship in summer 2026, funded by the faculty of engineering. He worked on investigating thermal interface materials (TIMs) for battery cooling and how consistently they performed. He designed a testing setup and tested various different TIM solutions to determine their thermal conductivity and variation across different samples. The results successfully confirmed the hypothesis that thermal interface pads have the lowest standard deviation and are therefore more consistent.
