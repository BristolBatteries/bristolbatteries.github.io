Daeyeun worked on numerical modelling of phase change materials for battery thermal management in electric flight applications, contributing to the BATMAA project and leading the authorship of a jounral publication. He went on to complete an MPhil in Nuclear Energy at the University of Cambridge and became a  Research Staff member in the School of Electrical Engineering at Korea University.
