## Overview

This Faraday Institution Industry Fellowship developed a testing framework for battery model parameterisation with exceptional thermal control, delivered in partnership with Thermal Hazard Technology and Imperial College London. The project addressed a key challenge in battery modelling: conventional testing methods can be slow and are often affected by poor thermal control, which reduces confidence in the parameters used to predict battery performance, heat generation and lifetime.

The work showed that faster testing procedures can produce high quality model parameters when combined with precise isothermal control. This included accelerated kinetic parameterisation, where AMPP was shown to compete with established GITT based methods in a much shorter test time, and thermodynamic parameterisation, where improved entropy coefficient measurements support better modelling of reversible heat generation. The fellowship also helped define performance requirements and testing procedures for Thermal Hazard Technology’s isothermal battery calorimeter, linking academic method development with future commercial battery testing capability.
