## Overview

This project used degradation testing as a basis for nurturing a partnership between the University of Bristol and CEA, the French Alternative Energies and Atomic Energy Commission. The project funded a mixture of the partnership-building, knowledge exchange, and feasibility studies, to lay the foundations for a joint EU Horizons bid.
Throughout its lifetime, a battery will see a progressive decrease in its ability to store energy and deliver power. This so-called cell degradation is strongly influenced by the electrical and thermal conditions a cell experiences. The project therefore cycled cells in various thermal configurations, to provide proof-of-concept data for building future joint bids between Bristol and CEA.
