## Overview

This project explored how battery degradation affects the performance and characterisation of second life electric vehicle battery packs, working with one of the UK’s leading second life energy storage companies. The work focused on the challenge of combining packs from different vehicles and chemistries, where differences in cell type, pack architecture, usage history and degradation state make reliable performance prediction difficult. Testing on degraded Tesla cells produced useful results, showing how real world ageing can influence cell behaviour and highlighting the importance of robust modelling and diagnostics when assessing packs for new life energy storage applications. Overall, the project was successful in generating valuable insights, while also demonstrating that further work is needed to develop scalable methods for comparing, grading and integrating diverse used EV battery packs.
