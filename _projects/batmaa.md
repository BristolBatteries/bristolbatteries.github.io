## Overview

This project explored whether phase change materials could provide a lightweight, passive thermal management solution for batteries in electric aviation. The work was motivated by the challenge that aircraft batteries operate in demanding thermal conditions, where cold ambient temperatures, high cooling rates and variable power demand can reduce accessible battery energy and limit flight range.

The project showed that while phase change materials offer an elegant concept, they are unlikely to provide a complete or realistic solution for aviation battery thermal management using current material properties. However, the work provided valuable design insight, identifying the key material properties that future phase change materials would need to achieve if they are to become viable in battery powered flight. This led to a publication setting out how phase change materials should be designed and assessed for future electric aviation applications.
